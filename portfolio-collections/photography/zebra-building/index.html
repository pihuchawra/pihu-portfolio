Zebra Building

After editing my original photograph of this building, I decided to tweak it to bring out a 3D effect in it. I used Procreate for the first time and played with the composition flow of the exterior. I wanted it to have a more dramatic pattern, almost like a zebra. To add a little more texture, I inserted a glitch, giving the final image a stylized and “trippy” look.
