Escapism

Medium
Willow charcoal, Charcoal powder

This piece was an experiment with charcoal. I wanted to see how well charcoal could spread like a powder, so I used newsprint paper, charcoal powder, and soft willow charcoal. I started with a few random streaks and brushes of powder but soon found the form it started to take. I went with the flow in this piece, adding and subtracting soft charcoal in places I felt most needed it. The face that emerged seemed to be escaping the darkness behind.
