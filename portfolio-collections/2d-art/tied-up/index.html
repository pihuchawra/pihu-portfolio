Tied Up

Medium
Charcoal, paint marker, thread

This piece shows the release of hope being tied down by control. The bird represents hope and positivity and conveys a longing for freedom and optimism in life. The person has let go of a bird due to external constraints. The red string tightly binding the hands represents the restrictions and control on the person, rendering them immobile.
