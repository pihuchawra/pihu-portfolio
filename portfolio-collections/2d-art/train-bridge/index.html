Railroad Bridge

Medium
Watercolor

I found a secluded perch on a hike in the middle of the woods where I had a clear view of a railroad bridge next to the walking bridge. This piece felt very personal to me because it was right after I went swimming at the nearby lake. The whole experience was calming: hiking silently among redwoods, swimming in an open lake with my friends, and enjoying the woods with them.
