Xacto Knife Study

Medium
Graphite
Dimensions
9" x 12"

I chose the nearest object on my worktable and placed it in multiple angles. I switched up each piece’s composition; taking apart the Xacto knife and sketching the parts. I used different blacknesses of pencils to identify a focal point.
