Feather

Medium
Ceramics, acrylic paint, paint marker

I'm very passionate about this creation as it spreads awareness about the significance of Columbus Day becoming Indigenous People’s Day, connecting with the Native American community. I created a plaque featuring a large feather, showcasing the names of many Native American tribes in California that were forcefully relocated. I placed many names on the regions they came from (northeastern tribes in the northeast corner of the plaque, etc.). I chose the feather because, in many Indigenous cultures, feathers symbolize prosperity, wisdom, and virtue; they decorate jewelry, blankets, and art.
